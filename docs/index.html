Why this board exists

The Monthly Table is a neighbor-built calendar — it points toward the work of two organizations already active around Las Cruces, and gives anyone a way to add a grassroots event alongside them.

In-home & community care

21st Century Care

A Las Cruces provider of home- and community-based care for seniors and people with disabilities. The care days on this board are organized by neighbors in that same spirit — check-ins, wellness visits, and support for caregivers.

Hunger relief

Roadrunner Food Bank

New Mexico's largest hunger-relief organization, moving food to pantries and distributions across the state, including the Mesilla Valley. Food drives posted here help stock those shelves ahead of the next mobile pantry.

Host one

Put your event on the table.

Anyone can post — no account needed. It shows up on the board for the whole neighborhood as soon as you submit.

  • Pick a date, time, and a place people can actually find.
  • Say how many hands you need, if any.
  • Set a 4-digit code so you can edit or cancel it later.
  • Leave a way for people to reach you if plans change.
Only you'll know this — use it later to edit or cancel the listing. There's no reset if you lose it.

Posted events are visible to everyone who opens this page — please don't include anything private.
